# ReArc: Concept Directions and Trademark Differentiation

ReArc = **Re** (rebuild, again, open; also *reverse engineering*) + **Arc** (geometry, orbit, continuity). The live mark is the **Sunrise** mark described in `BRAND.md`. Earlier explorations are kept in `explore/` and `options/` for history.

## 1. Three directions

### A. Sunrise (adopted)
A thick, round-capped horizon arc sits in front of a vermillion sun that rises from behind it. **Arc** is the horizon, a literal arc; **Re** is the sun that rises *again* every day. Only the part of the sun above the arc is drawn, so the mark reads as a stable, flat, two-part pictogram. It keeps the chunky, soft-cornered weight of a Mac app icon without needing a letterform, and it is the only direction whose silhouette (a dome over a band) stays recognisable at 16 px.

### B. Cyanotype Compass (`options/concept_a_compass.svg`)
A drafting compass draws the arc. The two feet of the compass form the colon in "Re:Arc", and the arc runs from a blueprint-style wireframe on the left into a solid bar on the right: the traced original becoming the rebuilt version. It carries the reverse-engineering story best, but it needs fine hairlines and a gradient of rendering styles, which fail at favicon size and are hard to keep single-colour.

### C. Sextant A (`NAVIGATION-LINEAGE.md`, `options/option-d-sextant/`)
A sextant-style capital A: two legs are radii of a navigation sector, the crossbar is the limb arc struck from the apex pivot, with calibrated clearance between parts to show the instrument "taken apart". It connects the Netscape wheel → Safari compass → Arc → ReArc lineage. It was dropped because a capital A built from an arc crossbar is exactly the idea Arc's own icon is built on, which is the closest we could get to the trademark we must avoid.

## 2. Why Sunrise

It scored best on the three criteria that matter for an icon: small-size legibility, flat single-pass reproduction (full colour, one colour, one-bit), and a concept that explains the name in one glance. The compass is richer but fragile; the A is the most confusable.

## 3. Differentiation from Arc (read this before shipping)

Arc's identity is a soft rainbow/pastel gradient with faux-3D ribbon shading, and an app icon built as a capital **A** from overlapping bars.

| Dimension | Arc | ReArc Sunrise |
|---|---|---|
| Concept / silhouette | Letter A: peaked apex, two legs, arc crossbar | Dome (sun) over a single arch band; no letterform, no apex, no legs |
| Colour | Pastel-to-saturated multi-hue gradients | Three flat colours plus one overlap tint; no gradients anywhere in the mark |
| Rendering | Pillowy, glossy, translucent layers | Flat, opaque, geometric; no blur, glow or shadow on the mark itself |
| Tile | Light tile with 3D-ish ribbons | White tile with a soft shadow (app icon only) |

**Honest risk assessment.** The Sunrise mark was developed after studying Arc's icon at the project owner's request ("use the current icon of Arc Browser … and think about Re:Arc"). It is deliberately not an A, not a gradient, and not a swirl, so it clears the original brief's explicit bans. But it does share a **family resemblance** with Arc's icon: a heavy blue arch band with a darker navy end, a darker overlap tint where two shapes cross, a warm red-orange shape behind it, and a white tile. A casual viewer could plausibly think "same style of icon". We have not had this reviewed by a trademark professional. Before a public release:

1. Get a trademark review (or a quick similarity search) of the mark and the icon together.
2. If any doubt remains, the cheapest fixes are, in order: (a) drop the navy-end / overlap tint, so the arch is a single flat colour; (b) move the sun off the vermillion/blue pairing (for example amber over a deep teal arch); (c) drop the white tile in favour of a dark tile.
3. Arc's own icon was used as a private reference during exploration; the copy has been removed from `brand/`. Do not add it back to the repo.

## 4. Construction

Everything is drawn on a 1024-unit grid inside a 512 viewBox (scale 0.64, centred).

- Arch: two arc segments of radius 380, stroke 130, round caps. Navy left piece, ultramarine right piece, overlap tint `#1627C8` where they meet.
- Sun: circle r = 225 at (512, 480), clipped so nothing shows below the arch.
- Mono variant: the arch is knocked out of the sun with a gap of 48 units per side so the two parts stay separate at 16 px (it was 22; that collapsed to a blob).
- Generated by `explore/gen/export_marks.py`; change constants there, not in the SVGs.
